Customer Retention Analysis - Internship Task 2

Hi! I am working as a Data Science Intern at Future Interns. In this second task, I analyzed a Telecom dataset to understand why customers are leaving the service (Churn) and what factors actually matter for keeping them.

What I did in this project:
First, I cleaned the data (especially the Total Charges column which was in the wrong format). Then I used Python libraries like Pandas, Seaborn, and Matplotlib to find patterns.

My Main Findings:
Contract Type is Key: This was my biggest find! People with Month-to-month contracts are leaving the most (1600+ people). Those on 2-year contracts are very loyal.
Gender doesn't really matter: I checked the numbers for both male and female customers, and the churn rate is almost the same for both. So, gender isn't the reason people leave.
Tenure: The average time a customer stays is around 32 months. Most people either leave very early or stay for a very long time.

How to run the code:
Open the .ipynb file in Google Colab.
Upload the Telco-Customer-Churn.csv file.
Run all the cells to see the graphs and analysis.

Based on the data, here is what I suggest the company should do:
Target Month-to-Month Customers: Since they leave the most, the company should offer them a small discount if they switch to a 1-year or 2-year plan.
First 6 Months Strategy: The tenure graph shows many people leave early. We should give "New Customer Bonuses" or special support during the first 3-6 months to make them stay.
Long-term Rewards: People on 2-year contracts are already loyal. We can give them "Loyalty Points" or free upgrades to keep them happy so they never think of leaving.
Auto-Pay Incentives: Often, people forget to pay and then leave. Offering a discount for setting up "Auto-Pay" can help in keeping them connected
